01Billboards

Marblism on the road into Midtown and Downtown for 4 weeks. One message: your AI employees, plus a QR code that books a seat at the next hangout.

  • 2 digital bulletins on the Downtown Connector (I-75/85) or I-85 near Midtown. Rotating spot, 4 weeks.
  • 4 street-level digital posters near the 4 hangout coffee shops, so the billboard and the hangout live on the same block.
  • Paid with Brex points. We put the billboard charges on the Brex card and redeem points as statement credit against them. Cash out of pocket drops by up to $11,000.
  • Creative made in house with Higgsfield and Sonny. No agency fee.

Vendors to quote: Outfront, Lamar and Clear Channel Outdoor (all sell Atlanta inventory). Confirm the points balance and the redemption rate in the Brex dashboard before booking.
