How Brex points pay for it

Caasie and the billboard owners don't take points directly, so we do it in two steps:

  1. Pay with the Brex card. All $23,500 of billboards and screens.
  2. Redeem points as cash back into Brex Cash at 1 cent a point, and use it to pay the card bill.

Covering all $23,500 takes about 2.35M points. If we have fewer, the rest is cash. Best case, cash needed is about $4,000 (the hangouts).

Where the prices come from: screen prices are Caasie's listed price per play for each screen. Static billboards in Atlanta run $1,500 to $8,000 per 4 weeks (AdQuick, Blue Line Media, DashTwo). Off-interstate boards sit at the low end. Final price comes from the vendor.
